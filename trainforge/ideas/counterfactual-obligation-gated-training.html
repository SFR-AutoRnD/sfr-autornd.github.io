Qwen3-4B · hard-math training

Counterfactual Obligation-Gated Training

Learn an operation’s boundary of validity, not just its surface pattern.

Internal novelty screen8.0/10 · confidence 0.69

01 · Thesis

Make legality—not frequency—the supervised signal.

Worked solutions repeatedly divide, cancel, square, take roots, apply logarithms, and simplify modular expressions. Standard next-token training rewards the visible operation, but does not isolate the premise that made it legal.

The same local operation should receive opposite preferences when its mathematical obligation is established and when that obligation is false or unresolved.

The method constructs a matched crossover pair for each admitted operation. One context proves the needed condition and permits APPLY; the other withholds or violates exactly that condition and requires DEFER/CHECK. This prevents the easy shortcut of teaching the model to become globally more cautious.

02 · Exact novelty delta

A local difference-in-differences intervention.

Process supervision and critical-token weighting can identify consequential reasoning steps. The proposed delta is narrower: intervene on the side condition while holding the local operator fixed, then optimize the crossover in likelihood between legal and illegal contexts.

The internal screen considers that combination—same-operator matched interventions plus a difference-in-differences objective—a strong novelty hypothesis. The score is not a publication claim; an independent external search and expert review may still find equivalent prior work.

03 · Real-data construction

Four fail-closed steps from proof to matched gate.

No Omni-MATH or AIME item is used to construct, select, or tune the training pairs.

01

Fix the hard corpus

Choose 160 unique Hendrycks-MATH Level 4–5 training problems, enriched for checkable operations while retaining category balance and excluding every held-out target row.

02

Extract obligations

Locate divide/cancel nonzero conditions, sign and extraneous-root conditions, log and square-root domains, and modular invertibility or coprimality requirements.

03

Build the crossover

Keep the operator and nearby language fixed. Establish the premise in the legal arm; withhold it or substitute an exactly invalid parameter in the paired arm.

04

Certify before training

Require exact symbolic checks, at least 100 pairs from 70 problems, and a 40-pair manual audit with at least 95% legality correctness and no malformed context.

04 · Objective

Reward sensitivity to the premise.

dL = log P(APPLY | legal) − log P(DEFER | legal)

dI = log P(DEFER | illegal) − log P(APPLY | illegal)

Lgate = −log σ(dL + dI − margin)

Canonical solution cross entropy remains active. Add one certified gate pair per problem with an initial gate weight of 0.1.

05 · Training contract

Small, matched, and reloadable.

  • Qwen3-4B LoRA rank 32, alpha 64, dropout 0, bf16
  • AdamW near 1e−4 for 20–30 real optimizer updates
  • Same rows, order, seed, token budget, and forward-pass count in control
  • Checkpoint, adapter, data, and code identities hash-bound
  • 32K response budget inside a 40,960-token context

06 · Deployment

The intervention ends at training time.

  • Reload the adapter on the unchanged Qwen3-4B base
  • Use standard text decoding and the existing answer parser
  • Add no Python, search, retrieval, calculator, or verifier
  • Add no special APPLY/DEFER interface at inference

08 · Failure modes

Reasons to stop before or after training.

  • Deleting a premise may make the prose unnatural and create an easy stylistic cue.
  • Exact checks may cover too few operation classes to support a useful adapter.
  • The model may learn excessive caution and defer operations that are actually legal.
  • A gain on the construction families may not transfer to diverse held-out hard math.
  • External review may identify equivalent paired-legality training and lower the novelty assessment.
