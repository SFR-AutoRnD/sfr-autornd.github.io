Research hypothesis

Supervising the first strategy switch that causally improves a trajectory should teach the model when to abandon an unproductive research path.

Complete idea

Data, tools, training, and evaluation

The implementation contract covers all four surfaces.

01

Data design

Eight-rollout groups containing bounded counterfactual conditions around candidate strategy switches.

02

Tool use

The underlying research-tool environment is held fixed so the experiment isolates supervision of the switch.

03

Training design

Span-level distillation masks the prompt, fetched pages, and unrelated turns; only the selected beneficial switch contributes to the objective.

04

Evaluation design

Frozen n=100 quality screen against the untouched incumbent, evaluated from the saved global-step-20 checkpoint.

Evaluation result

No promotion

The treatment was compared with its declared baseline on the frozen held-out set. The decision follows the measured sign of the result.

Fidelity and evaluation controls

What had to remain true

  • Counterfactual conditions fit inside each eight-rollout group
  • Only the selected assistant span receives supervision
  • Checkpoint identity is bound to evaluation
  • Non-positive held-out evidence cannot trigger promotion
