How autonomous training works

The loop runs itself. Every handoff stays controlled.

TrainForge owns the complete pipeline: data, tools, training, and evaluation. Novelty, implementation fidelity, compute, and promotion are enforced as gates.

Main contributions

Autonomous training with research-grade control.

Four enforced mechanisms connect a research idea to the exact model artifact that may be promoted.

01
Novelty

Discover new research directions

Scouts search across research domains while proposer and critic agents challenge every candidate against prior work and run history.

search → propose → challenge
02
Fidelity

Turn ideas into faithful code

A frozen contract covers data, tool use, training, and evaluation; structure, live-path, and behavior checks enforce it before training.

contract → code → verify
03
Efficiency

Focus compute on the strongest ideas

A staged funnel rejects weak candidates cheaply, raises fidelity for survivors, and reserves final confirmation for the strongest ideas.

f0 screen → f1 verify → f2 confirm
04
Trust

Promote only verified gains

Every held-out score stays bound to the checkpoint that produced it. Stale, invalid, or non-positive evidence preserves the incumbent.

evaluate → bind → promote or preserve
