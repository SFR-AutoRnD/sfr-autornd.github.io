Research hypothesis

Masked target-only supervision through LoRA on the attention projections can improve frozen exact-match route accuracy without modifying the base model.

Complete idea

Data, tools, training, and evaluation

The implementation contract covers all four surfaces.

01

Data design

A fixed routing task with target-token supervision and a frozen 120-example held-out split.

02

Tool use

Four internal research-tool route codes; no evaluator access during training.

03

Training design

Qwen3-4B, rank-8 LoRA on q/k/v/o projections, target-only cross-entropy, 20 effective updates on one H200.

04

Evaluation design

Reload the saved adapter, rerun the frozen held-out set, and require the reloaded score to reproduce the in-memory result.

Evaluation result

Promoted

The treatment was compared with its declared baseline on the frozen held-out set. The decision follows the measured sign of the result.

Fidelity and evaluation controls

What had to remain true

  • Frozen held-out split before training
  • Prompt tokens masked from the training objective
  • Full-parameter training forbidden by the executable contract
  • Saved adapter reloaded before the promotion decision

Interpretation boundary

What this result does not claim

  • This is the reliable guided platform demonstration, not a claim that AutoResearch autonomously discovered a novel training algorithm.

  • The recorded demo documents 20 effective updates; the current standalone LLM engine defaults to 40, so the frozen run contract—not a software default—is authoritative.
