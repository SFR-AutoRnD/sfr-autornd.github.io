GPT-OSS-20B · long-horizon research training

Cross-Fitted Source-Intervention Policy Optimization

Learn which evidence changed the answer—not which citation merely looks supportive afterward.

Independent internal screen8.8/10 · confidence 0.66
Novelty status
External review pending; not a publication claim
Core intervention
Cross-fitted source discovery plus randomized source visibility
Tool surface
Unchanged search, browser, and research tools

01 · Thesis

Turn source reliance into a causal training signal.

A correct research answer can carry plausible citations while depending on an unsupported clue, one publisher ecosystem, or parametric memory. Citation correctness therefore does not establish that the cited evidence caused the answer.

Randomly alter which independent source family is reachable, measure the resulting answer change, and reward reliable use or rerouting only when the intervention actually moves evidence acquisition.

The method separates discovery from falsification across publisher-owner folds. Its dense credit is derived from upstream access interventions; the final answer remains scored by exact task outcome.

02 · Exact novelty delta

From attribution after retrieval to intervention before retrieval.

Prior work audits whether an answer is supported, attributes predictions to retrieved documents, or rewards provenance. The proposed object is different: a cross-fitted instrumental estimate of causal source reliance inside a live multi-turn research policy, with explicit redundant-source rerouting and insufficient-evidence strata.

The internal screen found no reviewed primary method combining randomized pre-retrieval availability, independent publisher folds, a first-stage instrument gate, and policy optimization over 10–32-turn research traces. That is a provisional assessment, not proof of priority.

03 · Real-data construction

Fresh web tasks, independent source ecosystems, no benchmark leakage.

Official FRAMES, GAIA, HLE, and BrowseComp questions, answers, entity chains, and URL sets remain outside training and method selection.

01

Author fresh tasks

Create 160 human-audited, exact-answer-verifiable research tasks balanced across multi-source synthesis, mixed tools, expert lookup, and entangled identification.

02

Freeze source identity

Record URL, fetch time, content hash, publisher ownership, and syndication relationships for 4–8 live pages spanning at least three owner clusters.

03

Cross-fit discovery

Hash-split owner clusters into folds A and B. Discover on A and verify on B, then reverse the direction, keeping folds independent.

04

Randomize access

Within the verification fold, boost or slot-preserving suppress one decisive source family. Keep only interventions that change acquisition by at least the preregistered first-stage threshold.

04 · Objective

Credit evidence only under a strong instrument.

ρ̂d = [E(Y|Zd=+1) − E(Y|Zd=−1)] / [E(Ed|Zd=+1) − E(Ed|Zd=−1)]

use ρ̂d only when |ΔEd| ≥ 0.20

Assign the stop-gradient estimate to the first acquisition turn for source family d, then zero-sum normalize across the trajectory before adding it to terminal exact-outcome advantage.

05 · Training contract

Dose-matched policy optimization.

  • GPT-OSS-20B with the validated LoRA and Reinforce++ stack
  • Exactly 20 optimizer updates, eight unique task groups per update
  • Four trajectory slots: two fold directions × boost/suppress
  • Control sees the same masks and pages with hash-permuted intervention labels
  • Identical task IDs, seeds, rollout slots, optimizer-visible tokens, and adapter settings
  • Maximum 32 tool turns; exact-answer/outcome reward only

06 · Deployment

No new inference machinery.

  • Reload the adapter on the unchanged GPT-OSS-20B base
  • Use the existing Harmony roles and search/browser schemas
  • Add no token, model head, callable tool, source gate, or intervention at inference
  • Run ordinary native web search; the randomized visibility mechanism is training-only

07 · Closest work

Where the mechanism must distinguish itself.

  • Correctness is not Faithfulness audits support versus answer correctness
  • Faithfulness-QA probes evidence faithfulness
  • Source Attribution in RAG attributes outputs to retrieved documents
  • STAMP assigns provenance-aware research credit
  • DeepResearcher trains agents on real web interaction

Primary readout

Use official scorers and report accuracy/pass@1, tool calls, generated tokens, and latency. Separately report redundant-source rerouting, singleton-source abstention, citation support, and instrument strength on a frozen fresh-web set.

Required ablations

Remove cross-fitting, randomization, first-stage gating, ownership clustering, and fresh-fact filtering one at a time. A placebo-shuffled intervention must not reproduce the treatment effect.

08 · Failure modes

Reasons to stop the claim.

  • The visibility assignment may be a weak instrument.
  • Publisher syndication may masquerade as independent corroboration.
  • Rank perturbations may affect outcomes through mechanisms other than source acquisition.
  • The model may learn blanket distrust, over-abstention, or domain reputation shortcuts.
  • Live-source churn may invalidate supposedly matched trajectories.
  • A placebo intervention performing similarly would defeat the causal interpretation.
