Dyadic Interaction Credit

Credit the research subroutine—the query, clue, and verification span—not an isolated turn that only works in context.

Independent internal screen8.2/10 · confidence 0.57
Novelty status
External review pending; not a publication claim
Core intervention
Counterfactual perturbations of nested multi-turn intervals
Tool surface
Unchanged search, browser, and research tools

01 · Thesis

Research credit is often non-additive.

A discriminative query can be useless without the later page choice; the page choice can look useless without the final corroboration. Scoring individual turns independently can erase this interaction or assign the whole outcome to the last visible step.

Intervene on contiguous research spans, decompose their causal outcome effects across a dyadic hierarchy, and redistribute only the interaction-preserving credit.

The method treats a multi-turn subroutine as the unit of intervention, then maps its value back to optimizer-visible turns while preserving total trajectory advantage.

02 · Exact novelty delta

Counterfactual interval credit with explicit interaction residuals.

Existing approaches use gold-answer likelihood changes, provenance exposure, one-step interventions, Monte Carlo Shapley values, or generic replay. The proposed delta is a structured set of causal interventions on nested, contiguous research intervals plus an exact Möbius residual that isolates non-additive span interactions.

A marginal-matched shuffled-credit control receives the same sampled trajectories and credit histogram. This directly addresses the risk that an apparent credit-assignment gain is merely extra effective training dose.

03 · Real-data construction

Replay real research from immutable tool checkpoints.

Every branch uses real web responses. Training excludes all official evaluation prompts, answers, evidence graphs, and URL sets.

01

Run the root

Collect a research trajectory of at most 32 tool turns and preserve an exact Harmony checkpoint after every tool observation.

02

Build the interval tree

Partition tool turns into the whole trajectory, two halves, and four quarters: seven nested intervals with preregistered boundaries.

03

Intervene

Restart before interval I, resample exactly |I| decisions under the current policy and equal local quota, then continue on-policy to a terminal answer.

04

Verify comparability

Keep a branch only when common-prefix page hashes are unchanged and both root and branch receive exact binary outcome scores.

04 · Objective

Recover hierarchical interaction credit.

VI = Yroot − YI

ψI = VI − VI,L − VI,R

ct = Vleaf(t)/|leaf(t)| + ΣI⊃leaf(t) ψI/|I|

The construction preserves Σt ct = Vroot. Center turn credits before adding them to terminal Reinforce++ advantage so the method redistributes credit without changing its mean.

05 · Training contract

Eight slots in both arms.

  • GPT-OSS-20B with the validated LoRA and agentic rollout stack
  • Exactly 20 optimizer updates and eight unique task groups per update
  • One root plus seven interval interventions per prompt
  • Control generates all eight and receives a deterministic within-trajectory credit permutation
  • Identical task IDs, seeds, rollout tokens, masks, examples, and optimizer steps
  • Maximum 32 tool turns; terminal exact outcome remains authoritative

06 · Deployment

All branching disappears after training.

  • Reload the adapter on unchanged GPT-OSS-20B
  • Use ordinary Harmony messages and native research tools
  • Add no interval tree, branch runner, reward head, token, or tool at inference
  • Preserve the original 32-turn serving contract

07 · Closest work

The occupied territory around the hypothesis.

  • TRACE uses answer-likelihood-derived turn credit
  • STAMP uses provenance-aware trajectory credit
  • Causal Agent Replay intervenes on agent steps
  • Counterfactual Shapley Credit Assignment attributes individual components
  • Credit Without Ground Truth warns about hidden effective-dose confounding

Pre-training falsification

Use a planted-interaction task where success requires two separated search subroutines. The causal method must localize their containing interval more strongly than the shuffled-credit control.

Required readout

Report official accuracy and long-horizon strata at 10–15, 16–23, and 24–32 tool turns, plus interval-versus-step, residual-versus-raw, tree-depth, shuffled-credit, and page-hash ablations.

08 · Failure modes

Reasons to reject the causal story.

  • Live-web drift may make replay branches incomparable.
  • Resampling an interval may change too much downstream state for a local interpretation.
  • Binary outcomes may be too sparse for stable interaction residuals.
  • The eight-slot branch cost may dominate its benefit.
  • Credit magnitudes may become unstable under rare branch flips.
  • Failure on the planted-interaction validation blocks the training claim.
