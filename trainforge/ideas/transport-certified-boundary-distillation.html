Transport-Certified Boundary Distillation

Distill a research decision only when its advantage survives answer-preserving changes to the surrounding web context and its essential claims are bound to obligations owned outside the model.

Method at a glance Certificate first; supervision second.
Supervision unit
Boundary-local action span
Admission rule
Transport gate + obligation gate

01 · Training problem

Whole-trajectory distillation can copy a lucky accident.

A teacher action may look useful because one page, ordering, or incidental snippet happened to appear in its rollout. Cloning the whole trace then transfers the surrounding accident along with the decision.

This method narrows supervision to a local decision boundary and asks whether the decision remains useful when non-essential parts of the evidence context change.

02 · Learning principle

Teach decisions that transport.

A boundary action earns a distillation target only when its advantage stays positive across controlled, answer-preserving web perturbations and its essential claims discharge verifier-owned obligations.

03 · Training recipe

Compile a sparse set of certified teaching spans.

The certifier decides which spans may train the policy; it does not rewrite the target action.

Candidate boundaries

Cut the trajectory at a real decision

Extract local segments around a search, fetch, pivot, or answer-commit decision. Retain the pre-decision state, chosen action, necessary continuation, tool observations, and downstream advantage needed to replay the boundary.

Context pairs

Create answer-preserving perturbations

Construct alternate accessible web contexts that preserve the task answer while changing incidental ordering or evidence presentation. Every perturbation and its preservation check must be recorded outside the policy.

Transport gate

Recompute the local advantage

Replay the candidate decision in each paired context. Admit it only when the positive direction survives the declared perturbation set; do not average away a failed context after admission.

Obligation gate

Bind essential claims externally

An environment-owned ledger marks required claims as open, supported, or discharged. A model-authored assertion cannot close its own obligation or make an otherwise unsupported span eligible.

04 · Update sequence

From rollout to one masked update

  1. Locate a teacher decision with positive local advantage.
  2. Generate the declared answer-preserving context variants.
  3. Replay the same boundary action and recompute its advantage in every variant.
  4. Collect the essential claims introduced by the local continuation.
  5. Require both transport stability and verifier-owned obligation discharge.
  6. Apply distillation loss only to the admitted action span and necessary continuation.

05 · Objective and masking

Rejected context receives no imitation loss

  • The token mask is zero outside the certified local span.
  • A failed transport check removes the full candidate span.
  • An unresolved essential claim also removes the span.
  • Accepted examples can retain a bounded advantage weight.
  • Admission rates and rejection reasons are logged per boundary type.

06 · Training-time components

Auxiliary machinery stays outside deployment

  • Teacher rollouts supply candidate decisions.
  • A perturbation generator supplies paired contexts.
  • An advantage runner replays the local boundary.
  • An external verifier owns the obligation ledger.
  • The trained policy deploys with its ordinary search and fetch tools.

07 · Controls and fidelity

Separate selective supervision from extra compute

  • Match source trajectories, checkpoint, examples, and update budget.
  • Compare certified masking against unfiltered local distillation.
  • Verify answer preservation without target-label leakage.
  • Audit that obligation state is immutable to the policy.
  • Current behavioral evidence does not yet prove the authored gates ran faithfully.

09 · Implementation checklist

What must be inspectable in a faithful run

  • Publish perturbation types, preservation checks, and pass rates.
  • Expose the exact token mask for sampled accepted and rejected boundaries.
  • Show which external obligation closed each essential claim.
  • Keep performance, mechanism fidelity, and novelty as separate records.
