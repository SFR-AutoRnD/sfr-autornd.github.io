Research hypothesis

Binding lead promotion to exact recent observations should reduce lead abandonment and identifier fabrication without rewarding the tool call itself.

Complete idea

Data, tools, training, and evaluation

The implementation contract covers all four surfaces.

01

Data design

Assistant trajectories containing recent evidence-bearing observations, resolver candidates, and explicit confirmation outcomes.

02

Tool use

Crossref and OpenAlex resolve candidates; fetch_url must confirm the selected DOI or URL before it becomes evidence.

03

Training design

Outcome-only, assistant-only SFT after confirmation. Unobserved, expired, or candidate-only leads receive zero weight.

04

Evaluation design

Compute-matched LoRA arms on the frozen n=100 screen, evaluated from the treatment checkpoint rather than a served-model label.

Evaluation result

No promotion

The treatment was compared with its declared baseline on the frozen held-out set. The decision follows the measured sign of the result.

Fidelity and evaluation controls

What had to remain true

  • Lead must be an exact substring of one of the two latest evidence-bearing tool outputs
  • Lead time-to-live is at most two tool turns
  • Resolver candidates are not evidence
  • Calling the lead tool has no intrinsic reward
